The Brooklyn Synth & Pedal Expo is back, October 17–18, and it’s bigger than ever, with about 15% more brands than last year!

Our small-ticket experiment worked beautifully, so we’re sticking with it. Your $5–$25 ticket helps us keep exhibitor costs low and makes it possible for more independent builders to join the party.

The result: more builders, more gear, more discoveries, and more of the community we’ve been building since 2011.

Come play with hundreds of pedals and synths, meet the people behind them, and discover your next obsession.
