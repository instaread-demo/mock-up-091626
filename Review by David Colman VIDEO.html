Back in the 1950's and early 1960's, a small French manufacturer named Facel Vega (FV) created a stunning line of sporting luxury cars. The FV was designed to appeal to an exclusive moneyed clientele. These V8 Chrysler-powered chariots looked exotic, proved fun to drive but expensive to maintain. For a time FV reigned as the ne plus ultra of mobile haute couture. The name most often applied to this genre of transportation was the Gallic phrase "Boulevardier", which translates into English as "Fashionable." According to the Dictionnaire Larousse, the term Boulevardier also applied to FV's mostly male clientele, who were considered "Men About Town."

So here we are, more than 60 years later, discovering that Genesis (the luxury branch of Hyundai) has accurately recreated the concept of the Boulevardier - now available for a price ($81,495) to both fashionable men and women with the good taste and hard cash to buy Genesis's 2026 G80 3.5T Prestige Black AWD. In the spirit of its predecessors, the G80 is stunningly attractive, with a gracefully elongated snout, cleanly sculpted side panels, and a low roofline melding into a Kamm-style chopped tail. With its enormous mesh black grill leading the way, the G80 pounces on back road traffic like a shark dismantling prey. I have never experienced any vehicle that possessed the visual magic to shunt slower traffic into pullouts so quickly. In one 80-mile round trip up and down busy summertime California Route 1, at least 8 slower motorists took one look in their mirror at the G80, then utilized the next available pullout to let it pass. We never flashed our lights, honked our horn, nor followed too closely. There's no need for such crude forms of intimidation when you're wheeling the Prestige Black AWD on a crowded two-lane. One look in the mirror, and the seas part for you like magic.

It should be noted that Prestige Black applies only to the rather somber interior of this Genesis. Its exterior is painted sparkling metallic white, a shade Genesis calls UYUNI WHITE that will cost you an extra $650. So completely equipped is this four-seat luxury sports sedan that the paint charge is the only extra cost option puffing the window sticker. Fully equipped doesn't begin to cover all the luxury and convenience features that make road life with this Genesis just a little bit easier than you're conditioned to expect. The doors, for example, are soft-close, so no slamming necessary here. Same goes for the powered hands-free "Smart Trunk" with "Auto-Open." The graceful-looking automated front seats offer 4-way power lumbar support. They slide backwards automatically upon entry and exit to facilitate your access. The heated and ventilated Nappa Leather seats feature exclusive Prestige Black quilting. The driver's side bucket features a powered cushion extension, side bolster manipulation and an "Ergo-Motion" massage feature. Passengers in row two are treated to heated seats, and an ingeniously designed center armrest/drink caddy that's straight out of Business Class air travel. All occupants enjoy top echelon tunes courtesy of a standard Bang & Olufsen Premium Audio system.

This Genesis handles twisty back roads with competence, combining predictable handling with acceptable ride comfort. But this is not primarily a sports sedan. All those electrical assists and luxury enhancements add up to a rather ponderous curb weight of 4,608 pounds. Despite the stability provided by a quartet of 20-inch-diameter Black Alloy Wheels (shod with TW500 245/40R20 front/275/35R20 rear Pirelli P Zero all season radials), once the G80 sedan takes a cornering set, it doesn't like to deviate from its chosen course. That's because this is one big, heavy limousine. Handling is predictable, but the G80 does not achieve the exactitude and precision you would expect from a BMW or Audi sport sedan. One of the other shortcomings I discovered was lack of intuitive brake feel. The over-boosted pedal offers little resistance, so stopping transitions from minimal pressure to full panic without any intermediary warning feedback.



2026 Genesis G80 3.5T Prestige Black AWD

What this sedan does best is accelerate like a guided missile in a straight line. With its lovely-looking 3.5-liter twin-turbo V6 cranking out 375hp and 391lb.-ft. of torque, the G80 3.5T posts a 0- 60 mph run of 5.1 seconds, and cranks off quarter-mile sprints of 13.6 seconds at 105mph. Top speed is estimated at 155mph. In the best tradition of the Boulevardier, the G80 is primarily an interstate tool. Thanks to its endless amenities, it will cover huge daily travel expanses without tiring you or your passengers out. You will arrive at distant destinations just as comfortable and refreshed as if someone else had done the driving. If you're looking for a mileage shrinking tool without peer, this beautifully crafted Genesis should be your holy grail.
